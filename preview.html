🌱 Beginner guide — read this and you are dangerous

You need zero math. Follow the stones:

  1. Stones: 1 stone + 1 stone = counting. Group by tens → 47 = 4 tens + 7.
  2. Grids: 3 rows of 4 = 12. Turn it → 4 rows of 3 = 12. That is why 3×4 = 4×3.
  3. Undo: 3−5 = −2 (debts). A bar cut in 4 = 1/4. A square of area 2 has side √2 = 1.4142… never repeating.
  4. Balance: x+3=7 → take 3 from both sides → x=4.
  5. Machines: input → double+1 gives line; input² gives parabola.
  6. Triangles + waves: 3²+4²=5². A wheel rim height draws sine.
  7. Speed: curve x² has slope 2x right now.
  8. Distance: speed × thin time slices summed = area. Derivative and integral undo each other.
  9. Arrows + grids: arrow = [across,up]. Matrix moves all arrows at once. Photos and AI words are numbers in grids.
  10. Not knowing: 10 peg rows → 1,024 paths, 1 to far left, 252 to middle → bell curve.

Step-by-step check: each claim has a green test in experiments/run_all.py. If it prints ALL FLOORS VERIFIED, you understood it correctly.
